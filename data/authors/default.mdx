Hi, I'm John, and I want to improve how we educate children in Ireland on tech and cybersecurity before they posess a mobile phone. I am motivated by the desire to teach my own two young children in the future, and the belief that my experience and qualifications make me well-placed to achieve this goal.

Over the past 20 years, my career has been spanned two fields - education and technology. My parents were both teachers, and the apple doesn't fall far from the tree. I love the personal aspect of teaching - especially seeing .

On the education side, I have extensive experience designing and delivering curricula to children and adults in a range of contexts. From helping small groups of primary school ESL students learn grammar to lecturing hundreds of college students on technical subjects including <i>Language and Communication in the Digital Age (pictured)</i>, I have thoroughly enjoyed the process of making complex subject matter simple.

and understandable. On the technical side, I hold a PhD in Computer Science from UCD. I currently help provide state-of-the-art Speech and Language Technologies for the Irish language to the general public as part of TCD's [ABAIR]('hhtps://abair.ie') project. Recently I have been specialising in infrastructure and cybersecurity, gaining ISC2's [CISSP](https://www.isc2.org/certifications/cissp) certification in the process. I also lectured for two years at TCD, with one spent as Assistant Professor of Speech and Language Technologies.
