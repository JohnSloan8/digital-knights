Hi, I'm John, and I want to improve how we educate children in Ireland on tech and cybersecurity. I believe we should equip young people with an understanding of their devices, data, and digital footprint before they enter the digital world. However, this requires a much more comprehensive and technically-focussed curriculum than is currently available. The development of the Digital Knights curriculum is motivated by a desire to best prepare my own kids, and also to help other parents who would like to give their children a similar foundation.

My own career has been spent entirely in the fields of education and technology. From helping small groups of primary school ESL students in South Korea learn the basics of grammar (images 1&2), to lecturing hundreds of college students in Trinity College on subjects including <i>Language and Communication in the Digital Age</i> (images 3&4), I have 2 decades experience in curriculum development and delivery in a range of contexts. On the technical side, I hold a PhD in Computer Science from UCD, and ISC2's CISSP cybersecurity certification. I currently work as a Research Fellow at TCD, developing state-of-the-art Speech and Language Technologies for the Irish language as part of the [ABAIR]('hhtps://abair.ie') project. I have been invovled in third-level teaching for 10 years, with one spent as Assistant Professor of Speech and Language Technologies at TCD.

Through the years, I have been lucky to have had the opportunity to experiment with a variety of teaching methods. Some were successful, others not so much. I am a strong proponent of hands-on, practical, task-based learning in small groups. A structured curriculum is critical for providing a clear path towards achievable and desirable goals. The guidance of an expert is also crucial, as they can lead students along the path defined by the curriculum, while encouraging creativity and independent thinking to forge a path of their own. I have used all the skills and experience gained over the past 20 years in putting together the Digital Knights curriculum.

Outside of work, I am a husband, father of two, a keen runner and an even keener golfer. When not working or spending time with family, I can usually be found on the track at Dundrum South Dublin Athletics Club, or on the course at Stackstown Golf Club.
