Hi, I'm John, and I want to improve how we educate children in Ireland on tech and cybersecurity. This is motivated by a desire to prepare my children for entry into the digital world, and to help other parents who would like to give their own children a similar foundation.

I believe my experience and qualifications provide a strong foundation for developing and delivering an effective curriculum to achiave the goals of this project. I have spent the past 20 years in the fields of education and technology. From helping small groups of primary school ESL students learn the basics of grammar, to lecturing hundreds of college students on technical subjects including <i>Language and Communication in the Digital Age</i>, I have extensive experience in curriculum development and delivery. On the technical side, I hold a PhD in Computer Science from UCD, and ISC2's CISSP cybersecurity certification. I currently work as a Research Fellow at TCD, providing state-of-the-art Speech and Language Technologies for the Irish language to the general public as part of the [ABAIR]('hhtps://abair.ie') project. I have also lectured for two years at TCD, with one spent as Assistant Professor of Speech and Language Technologies.

Through the years, I have been lucky to have had the opportunity to experiment with a variety of teaching methods. I am a great believer in the effectiveness of hands-on, practical, task-based learning in small groups. A structured curriculum is necessary to create a clear path towards achievable, and desirable goals. The guidance of an expert is needed to lead students along this path while encouraging creativity and independent thinking for them to forge their own.

Outside of work, I am a husband, father of two, a keen runner and an even keener golfer. When not working or spending time with family, I can usually be found on the track at Dundrum South Dublin Athletics Club, or on the course at Stackstown Golf Club.
